Cookie Manifest

ENTECH Labs operates on a Zero-Persistence principle for general browsing. We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party behavioral cookies.

Essential Telemetry Only

To maintain system state and security, we utilize the following local storage protocols:

  • Session Integrity: Used to maintain your connection during secure transmissions.
  • Preference Persistence: Stores your acknowledgement of these protocols so you are not prompted repeatedly.
  • Terminal State: Local cache to ensure the R&D terminal simulation performs optimally on your hardware.

Note: Blocking these via browser settings may result in degraded terminal performance or failed contact submissions.

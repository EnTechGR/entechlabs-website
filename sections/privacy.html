Privacy Protocol

Data handling and transparency manifest for Entech Labs.

Last Updated: March 2026

At Entech Labs, we believe security is impossible without transparency. This protocol outlines how we handle the minimal data telemetry we collect during your engagement with our platforms.

1. Data Collection

We do not utilize third-party tracking cookies or invasive analytics. Our "Vektör" agent simulation is purely client-side; no terminal data or input strings are transmitted to our servers unless specifically submitted via the contact portal.

2. Encryption & Storage

Any data submitted via the Get in Touch interface is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. Information is stored in isolated, air-gapped environments for the duration of the research engagement and purged upon completion.

3. Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or lease telemetry data. We only disclose information if required by a valid legal warrant or to prevent an active, verified threat to our infrastructure.

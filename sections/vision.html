Our Thinking

Offensive knowledge is
the best defensive posture.

We are developers and researchers who operate under a simple premise: you cannot defend what you don't understand from the attacker's perspective. Every engagement, product, and paper we publish originates in adversarial thinking.

Our R&D focus is the emerging intersection of autonomous AI agents and security — studying how LLM-based systems can be manipulated, subverted, and weaponized, so we can build countermeasures before these patterns mature in the wild.

We operate transparently. Our tooling is open-source. Our CVEs are fully disclosed. Security through obscurity is not security — the community benefits when research moves in the open.
